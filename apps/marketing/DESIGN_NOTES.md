# Landing design decision

Selected direction: **Caderno de rota**.

The editorial split layout won because its “edital spine” makes the product’s central promise visible at a glance: the official document becomes a reviewed topic, then a material reference, then a task that fits today.

The production refinement keeps the academic navy, learning blue, progress teal, attention gold, paper white and berry palette. Its personality comes from the real vocabulary and constraints of Brazilian concurso study—edital publicado, páginas da aula, horas líquidas, revisão and questões—rather than decorative SaaS motifs.

The single signature interaction is the native, keyboard-accessible “Por que esta tarefa agora?” disclosure. A restrained marca-texto stroke appears on hover, keyboard focus and while expanded; reduced-motion preferences collapse the transition.
